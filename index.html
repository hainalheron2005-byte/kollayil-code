Government of Kerala

The Government of Kerala was formed on 1 November 1956, following the reorganisation of states on a linguistic basis. Kerala introduced a three-tier Panchayati Raj system to strengthen local self-government and promote people's participation in development. The system consists of Grama Panchayats at the village level, Block Panchayats at the intermediate level, and District Panchayats at the district level.
